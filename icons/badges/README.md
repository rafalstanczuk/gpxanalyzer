# Google Play badges

Official localized "Get it on Google Play" badges downloaded from Google
(https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/), transparent padding cropped and scaled to 400 px wide.
Use is governed by the Google Play Badge Guidelines (https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/):
the badge may only link to the app's Google Play listing and must not be altered beyond scaling.
The English badge on the root page is the SVG in `../GetItOnGooglePlay_Badge_Web_color_English.svg`.
