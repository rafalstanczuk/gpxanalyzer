New Features:
• Freehand Drawing — draw routes by dragging across the map; strokes are automatically simplified when you lift your finger.
• Bézier Curve Smoothing — smooth your drawn routes with adjustable tension and detail sliders in the new Drawing Tools dialog.
• Heatmap Overlay — visualize cached elevation data on the map as a color-coded grid, switchable between density and elevation coloring with source filtering.
• Elevation Settings — choose your elevation fetch strategy (API, cached, or cache-first), view cache statistics, and toggle auto-import of elevation data from GPX files.
• Drawing Tools Dialog — unified settings for curve smoothing, freehand simplification, and elevation sampling step, each with contextual help.

Improvements:
• Heatmap grid adapts to zoom level with stable rendering during pan and zoom.
• Elevation fetch supports progress preview, real-time updates, cancellation, and batch delivery.
• All drawing and elevation settings persist across app restarts.
• New on-map heatmap legend with color gradient, statistics, and source label.

Bug Fixes:
• Fixed freehand stroke cancellation when a second finger touches mid-stroke.
• Added safety checks for screen-to-geo coordinate conversions to prevent edge-case crashes.
